**Document ID:** WTH-001

**Module:** Weather System

**Version:** 1.0

**Status:** Approved

**Priority:** High

---

# 1. Purpose

The Weather System simulates dynamic weather conditions across Visakhapatnam.

Weather continuously changes over time and directly affects forests, water, agriculture, wildlife, citizens, buildings, disasters, and environmental health.

---

# 2. Objectives

The Weather System shall:

- Simulate realistic weather.
- Influence environmental systems.
- Affect resource production.
- Trigger natural disasters.
- Support educational learning.
- Remain deterministic.
