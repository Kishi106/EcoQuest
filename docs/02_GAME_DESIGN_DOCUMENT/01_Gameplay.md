# 1. Gameplay Overview

EcoQuest is a single-player browser-based environmental simulation game where the player develops and manages the city of Visakhapatnam (Vizag) while balancing environmental sustainability, economic growth, and citizen well-being.

Unlike traditional city-building games, the objective is not to maximize wealth or population. Instead, players are encouraged to create a healthy, resilient, and environmentally sustainable city through realistic decision-making.

The game emphasizes experimentation, observation, and long-term planning.

---

# 2. Core Gameplay Philosophy

EcoQuest is built around five core principles.

## Learn by Doing

Players discover environmental concepts through gameplay rather than tutorials.

---

## Every Decision Matters

Every action has positive and negative consequences.

Example:

Build Factory

Benefits:
- Jobs
- Tax Revenue

Consequences:
- Air Pollution
- Increased Disease Risk
- Wildlife Habitat Loss

---

## Long-Term Thinking

Some consequences appear immediately.

Others may take several in-game years before becoming visible.

---

## No Perfect Strategy

Every solution creates new challenges.

Players constantly balance competing priorities.

---

## Sustainability Above Expansion

Rapid city growth is never the primary objective.

Long-term environmental health is always more valuable than short-term profit.

---

# 3. The Two Gameplay Phases

EcoQuest is divided into two connected gameplay phases.

---

# Phase 1 — City Simulation

This is the main game.

Players develop Vizag while managing:

- Economy
- Environment
- Water
- Energy
- Agriculture
- Forests
- Wildlife
- Citizen Happiness
- Public Health

Time progresses continuously.

Players build, observe, and adapt.

---

# Phase 2 — Eco Challenges

Outside the city simulation, players participate in real-world environmental activities.

Examples:

- Plant a Tree
- Save Electricity
- Use Public Transport
- Avoid Plastic
- Clean Local Areas
- Participate in Recycling

Players upload proof.

Rewards include:

- Eco Points
- Achievements
- Certificates
- Leaderboard Progress

This phase encourages real-world environmental action.

---

The primary gameplay loop is:

Observe

# 9. Decision Making

Every player action modifies one or more simulation systems.

Example:

Cut Forest

↓

Forest Cover Decreases

↓

Wildlife Declines

↓

Pollination Reduces

↓

Crop Yield Drops

↓

Food Prices Rise

↓

Citizen Happiness Falls

↓

Economy Changes

Every major action should trigger a realistic chain reaction.

---

# 10. Progression

Players do not level up.

Instead, the city evolves.

Progress is measured through:

Environmental Health

Renewable Energy

Water Quality

Citizen Happiness

Public Health

Forest Coverage

Wildlife Diversity

Economic Stability

Achievement Completion

Educational Progress

Population:
5000

0%

Citizen Happiness:
90%+

Renewable Energy:
90%+

Water Supply:
Stable

Forest Cover:
Healthy

Wildlife:
Stable

Budget:
Positive

No Environmental Crisis

Maintaining these conditions demonstrates a sustainable city.

---

# 12. Losing

Players lose when recovery becomes impossible.

Examples:

Pollution exceeds 80%

Water below 40%

Budget becomes unrecoverable

Disease outbreak collapses population

Environmental collapse

Players may restart or load an earlier save.

---

# 13. Sandbox Mode

There is no fixed ending.

Players may continue improving the city indefinitely.

New environmental challenges continue appearing over time.

---

# 14. Replayability

Every playthrough should differ because of:

Different strategies

Different disasters

Different weather

Different city layouts

Different environmental priorities

Different policies

No two cities should evolve identically.

---

# 15. Difficulty Philosophy

EcoQuest does not rely on artificial difficulty.

Difficulty emerges naturally from the interaction of simulation systems.

Poor decisions create increasingly complex environmental problems.

Good planning creates resilient cities.
