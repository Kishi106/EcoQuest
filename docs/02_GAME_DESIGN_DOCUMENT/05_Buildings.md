---

# 1. Purpose

The Building System manages every structure constructed within EcoQuest.

Buildings provide housing, education, healthcare, employment, utilities, environmental services, and economic growth.

Every building affects resources, citizens, the environment, and the economy.

---

# 2. Objectives

The Building System shall:

- Support city development.
- Simulate realistic construction.
- Consume resources during construction.
- Require maintenance.
- Interact with every simulation system.
- Encourage sustainable urban planning.
