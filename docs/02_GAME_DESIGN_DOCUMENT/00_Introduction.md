# 1. Purpose

This Game Design Document (GDD) defines every gameplay mechanic, simulation system, rule, interaction, interface, and progression system that forms EcoQuest.

The purpose of this document is to provide a complete blueprint for developers, designers, artists, and AI coding agents so that the game can be developed consistently regardless of who implements each subsystem.

This document serves as the single source of truth for all gameplay-related decisions.

---

# 2. Game Summary

EcoQuest: Sustainable Future is a browser-based environmental simulation game where players redesign and sustainably develop the city of Visakhapatnam (Vizag), India.

Players begin with the city in its early development stage (default year 2000, configurable up to 2015). Through strategic planning and environmental decision-making, they guide the city's growth while balancing:

- Economy
- Environment
- Citizen Happiness
- Public Health
- Water Resources
- Renewable Energy
- Biodiversity

Every decision influences multiple interconnected systems, creating realistic long-term consequences.

Unlike traditional city builders, EcoQuest focuses on sustainability rather than maximizing profits.

# 3. Core Gameplay Loop

The player continuously repeats the following gameplay cycle:

Observe

↓

Analyze

↓

Plan

↓

Build

↓

Simulate

↓

Evaluate Results

↓

Improve

↓

Repeat

Each cycle represents the player's effort to create a more sustainable city while responding to changing environmental and social conditions.

---

# 4. Learning Through Simulation

EcoQuest teaches environmental science through interactive simulation rather than direct instruction.

Players discover concepts naturally by observing:

- Pollution spreading through urban areas.
- Forest loss affecting biodiversity.
- Water shortages reducing agricultural productivity.
- Renewable energy lowering emissions.
- Wildlife disappearing when habitats shrink.
- Climate change increasing disaster frequency.

Every gameplay mechanic is designed to reinforce environmental education.

---

# 5. Development Philosophy

EcoQuest follows four primary principles:

## Simulation First

The simulation engine drives the entire game.

Graphics, animations, and UI only visualize the simulation.

---

## Education Through Experience

Players learn by experimenting and observing the outcomes of their own decisions.

---

## Realistic Consequences

Every decision produces advantages and disadvantages.

There are no perfect solutions.

---

## Long-Term Thinking

Players are encouraged to think in decades rather than minutes.

Actions taken today may produce consequences many in-game years later.

# 7. Primary Objectives

The player should learn how to:

- Balance economic growth with environmental protection.
- Preserve biodiversity.
- Manage limited natural resources.
- Reduce pollution.
- Increase renewable energy usage.
- Improve public health.
- Respond to environmental disasters.
- Build resilient cities.

---

# 8. Win Philosophy

EcoQuest has no traditional ending.

Instead, players continuously improve their city.

The highest environmental certification is awarded when all sustainability requirements are satisfied, demonstrating that the player has successfully created a balanced and environmentally responsible city.
