# 1. Purpose

The Wildlife System manages all living animal species within EcoQuest.

Wildlife exists independently of the player and reacts naturally to environmental conditions, habitat quality, food availability, climate, pollution, and human activities.

Healthy wildlife populations improve ecosystem stability, biodiversity, tourism, and environmental health.

---

# 2. Objectives

The Wildlife System shall:

- Simulate realistic wildlife populations.
- Simulate food chains.
- Support biodiversity.
- React to environmental changes.
- Encourage habitat conservation.
- Teach ecosystem balance.
