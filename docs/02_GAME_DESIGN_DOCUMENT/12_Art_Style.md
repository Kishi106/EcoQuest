# Art Style Engineering Specification

**Document ID:** ART-001

**Module:** Art Style

**Version:** 1.0

**Status:** Approved

**Priority:** Critical

---

# 1. Purpose

The Art Style defines the visual identity of EcoQuest.

The game should feel calm, vibrant, educational, and alive while accurately representing the geography and ecosystems of Visakhapatnam.

The art style must support performance on modern web browsers without sacrificing visual quality.

---

# 2. Design Goals

The visual style shall be

- Clean
- Colorful
- Semi-Realistic
- Stylized
- Easy to Read
- Performance Friendly
- Timeless

---

# 3. Visual Style

EcoQuest uses a

**Stylized Semi-Realistic Low Poly**

art direction.

The world should feel believable without attempting photo realism.

Natural colors should be slightly saturated to make environmental changes visually obvious.

---

Entire city remains visible.
