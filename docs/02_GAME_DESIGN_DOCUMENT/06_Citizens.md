# 1. Purpose

The Citizen System simulates the population living within EcoQuest.

Citizens react to environmental quality, housing, education, healthcare, employment, resource availability, and city development.

Every citizen contributes to the city's growth while also consuming resources and producing waste.

---

# 2. Objectives

The Citizen System shall:

- Simulate realistic population growth.
- Respond to environmental conditions.
- Require housing and public services.
- Consume resources.
- Produce waste.
- Influence the economy.
