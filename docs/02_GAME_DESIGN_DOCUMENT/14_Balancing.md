# Game Balancing Engineering Specification

**Document ID:** BAL-001

**Module:** Game Balancing

**Version:** 1.0

**Status:** Approved

**Priority:** Critical

---

# 1. Purpose

The Balancing System ensures EcoQuest remains challenging, fair, educational, and realistic.

Every gameplay system must encourage sustainable city development without creating impossible or frustrating situations.

---

# 2. Objectives

The Balancing System shall

- Encourage long-term planning.
- Reward sustainable decisions.
- Penalize unsustainable development.
- Prevent exploitative strategies.
- Maintain consistent difficulty.
- Preserve educational value.

# 25. Balancing Rules

Rule 1

Every decision must have both benefits and costs.

---

Rule 2

No resource is unlimited.

---

Rule 3

No strategy should guarantee victory.

---

Rule 4

Environmental recovery always requires time.

---

Rule 5

Player mistakes should be recoverable.

---

Rule 6

Long-term planning should outperform short-term optimization.

---

Rule 7

Healthy ecosystems provide long-term economic benefits.

---

Rule 8

The simulation should always favor realistic environmental behavior over gameplay shortcuts.
