# EcoQuest Delivery Outcomes

## Playable isometric city world
- The city is visible immediately on startup in a true elevated/isometric 3D view.
- The scene includes grass terrain, hills, coastline, water, beach, roads, buildings, trees, parks and infrastructure.
- Camera pan, zoom and rotation work with mouse, touch and keyboard-friendly controls.
- Day/night lighting and subtle environmental animation are present.

## Main menu and setup
- Main menu contains working New Game, Continue, Load Game, Sandbox, Dashboard and Settings actions.
- New Game accepts city name, Easy/Normal/Hard difficulty, starting map and Campaign/Sandbox mode.
- Starting values are population 500, budget ₹5,00,000, low pollution, 60% happiness, plenty of water and 70% forest cover.

## Construction, environment, terrain and water tools
- Construction includes residential, commercial, industrial, roads, schools, hospitals, renewable energy and water treatment.
- Environment includes trees, parks, forest restoration, polluted-area cleanup and coastal ecosystem protection.
- Terrain raise/lower, water infrastructure and water management are usable tools.
- Policy controls include renewable energy, waste management, water conservation, public transport and environmental protection.
- Tool activation, placement preview, cost display, affordability prevention, selection info, feedback and cancel/undo placement work.

## Simulation and dashboard
- Population, income/expenses, pollution, happiness, water, forest cover and environmental index update from player decisions.
- Dashboard shows all requested metrics, objectives, recent events, eco tips and progress indicators.
- Objectives track population 1,000, happiness above 70%, lower pollution, forest cover 80%, sustainable index and positive budget.

## Save/load and delivery
- Save, Continue, Load, rename and confirmed delete work with local browser storage.
- Logic is separated from UI and no external API keys or paid backend are required.
- The project builds with the existing Vite pipeline and exposes its source in the managed project.
